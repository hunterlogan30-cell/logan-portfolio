Car wrap design is where everything started for me. In 2022, designing a wrap concept for a friend's car was the project that pulled me into graphic design. I had no formal training at the time, just Illustrator and a blank template....

Four years later, the Porsche 718 Cayman GT4 is where that craft stands in 2026. Designed for a friend who races out of Monticello Motor Club, the livery had to read at speed from the sidelines and hold up in the paddock. It does. The car has gone on to win multiple championships at the club and is still considered the most recognizable car on the grid, which for a wrap is probably the highest compliment there is.

I created an official poster to unveil the updated Chorizo 2.0 wrap alongside collaborating on the refreshed sponsor package for the new competition season.
